BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT
Project: S/4HANA Order-to-Cash Improvement
Version: 1.0

1. Business objective

The business needs to reduce order-processing delays and prevent sales orders from being released when customers exceed their approved credit limits.

2. Customer and sales-order validation

The system must validate that the customer master record is active before a sales order can be saved.

The system shall verify the customer's available credit before releasing a sales order for fulfillment.

If the order exceeds the available credit limit, the system must place the sales order on credit hold and notify the assigned credit manager.

An authorized credit manager must be able to approve or reject a blocked sales order and record a decision comment.

3. Pricing and approvals

The system must calculate the sales-order price using the active customer price list, contractual discounts, and applicable taxes.

A discount greater than 15 percent must require approval from the sales manager before the order is released.

4. Inventory and delivery

The solution shall check product availability for every sales-order line and display the confirmed delivery date to the sales representative.

When sufficient inventory is unavailable, the system should propose the next available delivery date.

5. Integration

After order approval, the solution must send the confirmed order to the warehouse management system through an API interface.

If the warehouse interface fails, the system must record the failure, retry the message up to three times, and alert the support team.

6. Audit and reporting

The system shall retain the user, timestamp, previous value, and new value for credit-release and discount-approval decisions.

Sales managers must be able to view a report of blocked orders, approval status, order value, customer, and aging in days.

7. Security and performance

Only users assigned the Credit Manager role may release an order that is blocked because of credit exposure.

The sales-order validation response should complete within three seconds for at least 95 percent of requests during normal business hours.
